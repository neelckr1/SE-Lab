## Lab 1: Requirements Engineering & UML Use Case Modeling

### Objective
To analyze the problem statement for the Campus Placement and Internship Pipeline, define unambiguous functional and non-functional requirements, formulate the Software Requirements Specification (SRS), and develop UML Use Case diagrams capturing the interactions between various stakeholders.

### Key Stakeholders & Actors
- **Student / Candidate:** Registers profiles, uploads resumes, tracks eligibility criteria, applies for job/internship postings, and accepts/declines job offers.
- **Company / Recruiter:** Posts job descriptions, defines eligibility cutoffs (CGPA, backlogs), reviews candidate applications, shortlists candidates, and conducts interview rounds.
- **Placement Officer / Admin:** Configures placement drives, validates student eligibility, approves recruiter postings, records offers, and generates placement statistics.

## Lab 2: Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira

### Objective
To translate Lab 1 functional requirements into an actionable Agile Product Backlog using Jira (Scrum template), create user stories with Fibonacci story pointing, plan sprints, and simulate sprint execution with burndown tracking.

### Jira Project Configuration
- **Project Name:** Campus Placement and Internship Pipeline
- **Board Name:** CPIP board
- **Project Key:** `CPIP`
- **Template:** Scrum

### Epics Structure
1. **Epic 1 — Student Profile & Eligibility Management:** Student profile creation, resume uploads, and automated eligibility rule validation (CGPA thresholds, active backlog limits).
2. **Epic 2 — Job & Internship Posting Management:** Recruiter job/internship posting creation, role specifications, deadline tracking, and drive scheduling.
3. **Epic 3 — Application & Shortlisting Workflow:** Student job applications, automated screening against eligibility criteria, and recruiter shortlist generation.
4. **Epic 4 — Interview Scheduling & Offer Acceptance:** Multi-round interview coordination, slot selection, offer letter dispatch, and student offer acceptance/decline recording.

### Sprint Simulation & Burndown
- **Total Backlog:** 39 Story Points across 4 Epics.
- **Sprint 1 (21 SP):** Focused on Epics 1 & 2 (foundational profile and job posting infrastructure).
- **Sprint 2 (18 SP):** Focused on Epics 3 & 4 (application workflows, interview scheduling, and offer handling).
- **Burndown & Velocity:** Both sprints closed on schedule with active progress tracked across `To Do` → `In Progress` → `Done`.
